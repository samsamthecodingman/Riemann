# Riemann — brag plan

**Format:** vertical 1080×1920, 30fps, ~22s. **Tone:** polished-playful (Macaron calm, not hype).

## Answers
- **What:** A reading assistant that turns any article, paper or brief into an abstraction tree you can zoom through, from a one-minute gist down to the original paragraphs.
- **For:** People (especially ADHD brains) who find long documents hard to start and easy to get lost in.
- **Different:** A continuous zoom dial, passage by passage, with your place pinned.
- **Best claim:** "Read anything at the depth you need."
- **Visual hook:** a wall of dense text that collapses into one sentence.
- **Real UI shown:** the phone layout of the real app (fixture doc "How the Internet Actually Moves Your Data"): home, gist, three zoom levels, map sheet.
- **Caption:** "Read anything at the depth you need. Riemann starts with a one-minute gist and lets you zoom in only where you look."

## Storyboard
| # | Time | Scene |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Hook | 0–3.0s | Wall of the fixture's real source text drifts up; "Hard to start." / "Easy to lose your place." lands on top. |
| 2 Reveal | 3.0–5.6s | Text wall collapses; Riemann wordmark + macaron dots; "Read anything at the depth you need." |
| 3 Paste | 5.6–8.6s | Phone rises with real home screen; text pastes in; tap on Build gist. Caption "Paste it in." |
| 4 Zoom | 8.6–15.6s | Real reader: gist → bullets → titled points → original, each on a "More +" tap; readout climbs 3% → 100%. Captions: "Start with a one-minute gist." / "Zoom in only where you look." / "All the way to the original." |
| 5 Map | 15.6–18.4s | Map sheet slides up. "See the whole document as a map." |
| 6 Outro | 18.4–22.0s | Riemann wordmark, "Built for an ADHD brain.", repo link. |

## Sound
Synthesized warm keys in F (Fmaj7–Am7–Dm7–B♭maj7) at 96 BPM, soft kick and shaker; taps pitched to the key on each More + press; airy noise swells on scene changes; soft chime at the reveal. Effects mixed under the music.
