## Notes

- **Strongest confirms**: claims 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 10, 11, 14, 15, 16, 21, 22, 25, 26, 27, 28 all landed on primary or clearly-identifiable publisher/PubMed/journal pages with numbers matching the research files closely. These are safe to cite as-is.
- **Claim 24 is the one real correction**: the ~28.6% single-doc hallucination figure is not in arXiv 2410.13961's own text (verified by direct full-text search of the HTML version). The synthesis should either drop the specific 28.6% number or find its actual primary source before using it — don't cite 2410.13961 as the source for that particular number, only for the multi-document 20–45%/75% figures.
- **Claim 12 (Kalyuga r=.66 / 3.8x)** and **claim 17 (Hornbæk & Frøkjær ~20% slower)** and **claim 19 (arXiv 2406.17744 "~50%")** are UNVERIFIABLE/CORRECTED not because they're wrong, but because paywalls/403s and imprecise search-snippet coverage prevented pinning the exact figures to primary text. The general direction of all three claims is corroborated by multiple independent secondary sources, so treat them as "probably right, number unconfirmed at this precision" rather than retracting them.
- **Claim 5 (Murphy et al. speed)**: minor wording correction — the paper's own framing is "no reliable cost up to 2x, cost specifically emerges at 2.5x," which is very close to but not identically phrased as "little/no cost up to 2x, costs above 2.5x." Functionally the same for design purposes.
- **Claim 7 (Bastani PNAS)**: note there is a published correction notice (PMC12403119) for this paper — anyone citing exact effect sizes downstream should check the correction before quoting numbers, even though the qualitative finding (harm without guardrails, GPT Tutor guardrail condition mitigates) is confirmed.
- **Claim 13 (Khan Academy)**: the *fact* of the 2013 retirement is confirmed via Khan Academy's own community/support pages, but the *reason* (simpler dashboard vs. Google Maps API deprecation) is only corroborated by a fan wiki — treat the "why" as MODERATE, the "that it happened, ~2013" as CONFIRMED.
- Several primary sources (ACM DL pages, ResearchGate/ScienceDirect direct article pages, one WUSTL PDF) returned 403/blocked/cert-expired errors during this pass — these are flagged individually above rather than silently upgraded to CONFIRMED from search-snippet paraphrase alone, per the instruction that secondary blog/aggregator repetition doesn't count as confirmation. Where I could cross-check a specific number across 2+ independent descriptions of the same primary study, I treated that as sufficient (e.g., Roediger & Karpicke's 61%/40%).
